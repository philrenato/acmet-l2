DEGREES

  • The Fine Arts Department at Joliet Junior Colleg offers a variety of courses in art, including: Painting, Drawing, Jewelry and Metalsmithing, Pottery, Weaving, Design, Art History, and Lapidary Arts.
  • The Fine Arts Department offers virtually all freshman and sophomore level art courses available at any four year college or university.
  • Two years of study at JJC will prepare you for transfer to a senior institution and will give you the opportunity to earn an Associate of Arts Degree.  A program may be planned to meet an individual student's needs.

For more information on Joliet Junior College, visit:  http://www.jjc.cc.il.us
