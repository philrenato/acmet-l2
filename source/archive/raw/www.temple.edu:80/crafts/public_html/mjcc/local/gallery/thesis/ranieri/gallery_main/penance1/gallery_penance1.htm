The Sacrament of PENANCE forgives the sins one commits during their life. This Sacrament is one of the healing Sacraments that can save a Baptized person from sin.

In my experience with Penance, I received private Confessions where I spoke through a pierced screen-like barrier. This piece shows the intimateness experienced when confessing ones sins.
