Lori Addis

Bio:

Lori Addis currently attends Tyler School of Art of Temple University, and is a senior in the Metals/Jewelry/CAD-CAM program. She has received the Temple University Academic Achievement Scholarship Award, and her work has been previously shown at: Juried Student Exhibition, Tyler Gallery, Tyler School of Art, Elkins Park, PA, Spring, 1999. Glass and Ceramics Student Exhibition, Tyler Gallery, Tyler School of Art, Elkins Park, PA Spring, 1999. Student Exhibition, Penrose Gallery, Tyler School of Art, Elkins Park, PA, Fall, 1996.

 

 

 

Statement:

The work I have produced throughout the Fall semester of 1999 has begun a series of explorations of ‘mechanisms’ and ‘movement’. These explorations have influenced my benchwork as well. I have ventured to discover unique ways of attaching jewelry to the body, through the exploration of the ‘mechanism’ and ‘movement’ and striven to create new relationships between the body and jewelry.
