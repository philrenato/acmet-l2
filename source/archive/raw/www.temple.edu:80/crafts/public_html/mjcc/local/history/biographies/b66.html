BIOGRAPHY:


Martha Glowacki


Martha Glowacki was awarded a B.S. Degree in Art Education at the University of Wisconsin-Madison where she later received her M.F.A. degree. She has exhibited in various selected shows nation wide. Glowacki also conducted several lectures and workshops since 1981. In 1982 Glowacki was awarded a fellowship for visual arts from the National Endowment for the arts.
