Kim Ann Fraczek

Bio:

Kim Ann Fraczek is senior at Tyler School of Art in the metals/ jewelry/

CAD/CAM

department. She is currently working on designs which integrate CAD/CAM

materials and flameworked glass. Upon graduation this May, she plans to

relocate to New York City and remain an active designer.

Statement

The two worlds which I feel encompass my creations are the world of heat and

the world of space. I have always loved working with glass due to the use of

fire, and the ability to create swirling fluid forms. I have just begun to

explore the world of space with CAD. CAD gives me the ability to understand

space on a new level while allowing me the freedom to create any form I

imagine. I am truly excited to delve into this realm further. I am hoping to

become fluent in both of these mediums and combine them completely in the

future.
