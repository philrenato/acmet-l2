Fear is an emotion that can prevent individuals from having healthy relationships. Feelings of inadequacy, guilt, fear of the unknown, rejection, and abandonment can lead to unhealthy behaviors such as lying, cheating, jealousy, and even abuse. My, "To Be or Knot to Be Unity Bands," was made with the intent to be a constant reminder to work towards defeating these fears. The ideas that the bands are more difficult to remove and are opposed in direction signify the struggle one can endure when working through these fears. To Be or Knot to Be Unity Bands CAD/CAM tangible, sanders wax, non- oxidizing sterling silver, 1.5" x .72 " x 1.8"
