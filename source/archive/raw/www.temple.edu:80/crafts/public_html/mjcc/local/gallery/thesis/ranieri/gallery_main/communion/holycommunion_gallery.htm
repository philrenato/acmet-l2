HOLY COMMUNION, is another Sacrament of initiation, which in my experience was received second. Only a validly ordained priest could consecrate the bread and the wine so they can become the Body and the Blood of the Lord.

This piece shows his divine love and sacrifice where the cross form represents God. The two forms which represent the body and blood, come out and back together on the paten.
