When one accepts the Sacrament of HOLY ORDERS, the grace from this Sacrament imprints an indelible character on the soul of the recipient and configures him to Christ. A priest is one who brings God to men, and who unites God with men. The Latin name for priest is "Pontifex" a bridge builder, because he bridges heaven and earth.

This piece brings together The Father, Son, and The Holy Spirit with one individual on earth, Ordaining him.
