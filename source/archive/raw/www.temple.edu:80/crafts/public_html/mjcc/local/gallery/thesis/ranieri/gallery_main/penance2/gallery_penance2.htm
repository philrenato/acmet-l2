The Sacrament of PENANCE restores the gift of God's grace after it has been lost by committing a sin. The Sacrament reconciles us with the church.

Again this piece deals with the experience one has when confessing his or her sins. Here another passable but more revealing barrier is protecting the individuals identity during confession.
