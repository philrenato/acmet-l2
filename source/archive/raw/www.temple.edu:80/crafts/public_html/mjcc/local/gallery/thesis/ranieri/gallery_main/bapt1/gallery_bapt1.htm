BAPTISM is the first of the initiation Sacraments received in the Catholic church. This healing Sacrament acts as a remedy for all sins.

This piece was the first of the Baptism series. One can be reminded of the Sacrament by it's font-like form, reminiscent of a common form found in a Catholic church, that would contain Holy Water.
