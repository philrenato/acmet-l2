I received my undergraduate education from Tyler School of Art, Temple University in 1994. I chose to continue my education, at Tyler, under the guidance of Stanley Lechtzin and Vicki Sedman. The six years I studied under Stanley and Vicki were the most valuable years of my life. This exhibition represents technical research and aesthetic growth that would not have been possible without the direction of my professors.

I am part of a group of students who have been researching the possibility of using CAD/CAM/RP to create innovative objects in the field of Metalsmithing. As a group, we are similar in that we utilize the computer to create. Lately, I have found that there are differences in what is considered to be a finished object. The definitions that are offered about my work, are my opinion. These opinions may not reflect that of my peers.

For more information about me, check out my resume and statement...then move onto the work.

All comments, good or bad, are welcome.
