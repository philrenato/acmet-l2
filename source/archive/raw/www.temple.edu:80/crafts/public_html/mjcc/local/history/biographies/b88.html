BIOGRAPHY:


Daniella Kerner


Kerner was born in Tel-Aviv, Israel in 1952. She studied at Sir George Williams University in Montreal, Canada and at Tyler School of Art, Temple University in Elkins Park, Pennsylvania. She is presently Adjunct Associate Professor of Metals at Tyler School of Art, Temple University. Kerner also directs the Weekend Art Workshop. Her work is represented in permanent collections in the United States and in Canada. She is the recipient of numerous awards and grants and has exhibited her work nationally and internationally since 1975. Daniella Kerner has given several public lectures and belongs to a number of professional organizations. More information on Daniella Kerner is available at Tyler School of Art, Temple University Crafts' Department website.
