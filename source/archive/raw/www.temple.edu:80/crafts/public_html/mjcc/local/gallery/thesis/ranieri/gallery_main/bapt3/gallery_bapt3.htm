      IN BAPTISM we are buried with Christ. We are submerged under the waters. The rite is celebrated by immersion. Water is also the symbol of life.

In this piece the three flowing forms are containers which again, represent the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit flowing through the Baptized individual. The piece could contain actual holy water in the three forms, to act as a reminder of this Sacrament.
