TYLER SCHOOL OF ART, TEMPLE UNIVERSITY  ANNOUNCES THE TIFFANY & CO. FOUNDATION GRADUATE RESEARCH FELLOWSHIP

 

DEADLINE FOR APPLICATIONS: May 30, 2003

 

The Tiffany & Co. Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship in the Metals / Jewelry / CAD-CAM Area of the Tyler School of Art has been established by a grant from The Tiffany & Co. Foundation. The grant also provides for the creation of a rapid prototyping research facility which includes a Z Corporation Z 406® Color 3D Printer.

           

We invite applications for this position which will provide the successful candidate with $52,000 in full tuition remission and stipends, over two years, beginning in September 2003.

           

The successful candidate for this position will assist Professor Stanley Lechtzin in developing suitable materials and procedures for applying the ZCast® Direct Metal Casting process to the vacuum and centrifugal assisted casting of jewelry and small sculpture.  

 

Qualifications: Must meet all qualifications for admission into the Tyler School of Art Graduate program including a BFA in metals/jewelry or equivalent major. Experience with the creation of computer-aided-design solids models preferred.

 

Application to include: Letter of application, resume, 20 examples of your work in 35mm slide or on CD and a college transcript.

 

Send Application to:

 

Professor Stanley Lechtzin

Tyler School of Art
