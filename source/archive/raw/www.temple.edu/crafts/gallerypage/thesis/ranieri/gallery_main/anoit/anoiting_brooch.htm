ANOINTING OF THE SICK confers grace and unites the suffering of a sick person to the sufferings of Christ. The Sacrament also prepares the sick individual for the transition into eternal life.

This piece shows how a holy figure such as a priest would cradle a sick or deceased individual. The priest would then bless the individual with several prayers confirming them into eternal life.
