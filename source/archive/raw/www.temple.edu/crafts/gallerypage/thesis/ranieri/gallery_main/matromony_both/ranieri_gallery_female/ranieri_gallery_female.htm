HOLY MATRIMONY, establishes an in dissolvable bond between a man and a woman. The bond could only truly be broken by death. The Sacrament is usually celebrated within a mass where the man and woman entering marriage confer the Sacrament to each other.

This piece would be worn by the female. The recessed cross form reminds the woman, visually of how she is accepting of the males love and commitment.
