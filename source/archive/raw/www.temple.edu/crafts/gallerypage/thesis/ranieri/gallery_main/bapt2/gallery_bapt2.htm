The Sacrament of BAPTISM permanently relates us to God. This Sacrament also imprints an indelible character on the soul of the recipient of Baptism.

In this piece the flowing form simulates how holy water could flow constantly into the font and back through the cross.  The suggestion of flowing water, down into the font in a constant flow, acts as a reminder to the recipient of the grace that occurs when one is Baptized.
