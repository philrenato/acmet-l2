      CONFIRMATION, was the last of the initiation Sacraments I received. I received this Sacrament after my first Penance. This Sacrament is conferred through the anointing with chrism on the forehead. This is done by a Bishop laying his hands on the one to be Confirmed, and through the words, "be sealed with the gift of the Holy Spirit"

Here in this piece, the figure which becomes the cross form reaches out its arms accepting the one who is to be confirmed to kneel before him.
