Rapid Prototyping               The Major Processes

         As part of my thesis research at Tyler School of Art I filmed, edited, and produced a thirty-six minute video documenting five of the major rapid prototyping technologies. In many cases I documented the  building of  my own work while visiting various service bureaus and universities. The video will serve as an educational tool for the students  of Temple University as well as other institutions internationally.          

     Fused Deposition Modeling                   Sanders Model Maker
               
              Z Corp 3D Printer                       Selective Laser Sintering
              
                                                                           

To view excerpts from the video click on the images to the right. You will need Windows Media Player. If using a modem connection you may choose to save the video to your local drive. For PC: right click on the image, then select "save target as", save the file in a specified directory and play the video from that location. For MAC: control click on the image, save the file in a specified directory and play the video from that location.
