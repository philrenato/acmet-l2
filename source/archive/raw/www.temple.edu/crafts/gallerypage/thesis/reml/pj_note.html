A phone jack with three lines (voice, modem, fax) for a home office. Based on the architectural symbol for a telephone hookup.

One phone line is never enough. Working at home, working over the internet, sending and receiving faxes . . . our phone lines are working harder than Mr. Bell ever imagined. In this wired age, perhaps no fixture in the home deserves special attention more.

Produced with Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM) in green ABS plastic.

18cm long and 11.5cm at its widest, the ribbed cone stands 3.5cm from the wall at its peak.
