Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder is a disorder that can interfere with an individual's ability to focus, follow verbal instructions, and impair academic and social functioning. Some may find themselves having poor organizational skills, having a high activity level, engaging in aggressive, reckless, impulsive, or disruptive behaviors . Many of these behaviors can lead to low self-esteem and affect the individuals' life on several levels. In some cases, medication is suggested along with behavior therapy. My Focus Device was designed to represent how difficult a task it can be to increase attention span.  Placed over the eye, the individual would utilize the magnifying glass that is set into the eyepiece. The user/viewer can witness how difficult  the act of concentrating can be for these individuals.  Focus Device sterling silver, rubber, glass, brass, and titanium 12" x 9" x  2"
