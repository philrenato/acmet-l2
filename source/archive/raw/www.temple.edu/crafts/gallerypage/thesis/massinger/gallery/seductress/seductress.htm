Grandiosity can be displayed by an individual with Bi-Polar disorder. They experience periods of extreme highs and lows.  In this frame of mind, the individuals may feel as though they are superior or perceive their status to be much more grandiose than in reality. During periods of mania,  individuals may feel a "high" and boost in self esteem or energy.  My "Seductress Collar" is a representation of how  individuals may feel in a state of manic grandiosity. The piece is to be worn around the neck, much like a peacock displaying its feathers.   Seductress Collar CAD/CAM, virtuall 14" x 9" x 13"
