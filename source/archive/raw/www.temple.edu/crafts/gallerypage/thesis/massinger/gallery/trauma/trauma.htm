Post traumatic stress disorder can affect many aspects of an individual's life. It can affect their relationships and the way in which they cope with everyday problems.  Behavior modification is a form of therapy in which an individual introduces different ways of dealing with old emotions, feelings, behaviors, situations, or belief systems.  Many people, who have been exposed to abusive environments, learn to live with the experience. Some may relive the experience in the form of a flashback. Some may find it difficult to talk about abusive or traumatic experiences. The microphone was intended to reference a "voice box" for these victims. An individual could record into the microphone, and if they chose,  place the microphone back into the mouth and press the playback button with their teeth.  Trauma Mic  Sterling silver, rubber, plastic, adapter 3.75" x  2" x 2"
