 The next two pieces are the “Gooze Bracelet”. The “Gooze Bracelet” consists of Gooze (a slippery, translucent, gel like polymer, composed mainly of water) and pointy parts giving pressure to the wearer’s arm by weight transfer. If the wearer does not maintain a steady balance, the Gooze will lean towards the side where it is inclined, causing pressure and pain to the arm.
