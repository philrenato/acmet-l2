This piece is the “BB Bracelet”. This concept is similar to the “Gooze Bracelet”, but in this case, instead of using Gooze, BB's are the substance that shifts the weight. Any movement of the wearer will cause the bracelet to come lose and fall.  The shift of the weight on the BB bracelet will be more sudden than the weight shift of the “Gooze Bracelet”, because of the substances’ nature. The wearer of the “Gooze Bracelet” will slowly experience pain because of the movement, however the “BB Bracelet” wearer will quickly notice loss of the bracelet with sudden movement.
