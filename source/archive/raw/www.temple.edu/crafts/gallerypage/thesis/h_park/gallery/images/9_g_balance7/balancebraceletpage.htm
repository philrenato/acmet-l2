Following the airplanes plowing into the buildings, all of us were wondering what was going to happen next.  As the buildings were tumbling down, we prayed and hoped that all of the people in the building were able to evacuate and wished that they all made it alive. The design of the "balance bracelet" and "the moment bracelet" derive from this feeling of dreadful anticipation.

This bracelet just hangs on the wrist secured by the weight of the bracelet and with just any movement; the bracelet will fall from the wrist.
