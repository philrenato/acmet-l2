Douglas J. Bucci

Bio:

Douglas Bucci is a Jeweler/Artist that obtained his MFA in 1998 from Tyler School of Art/Temple University in Philadelphia, PA. Doug is currently teaching Foundation 3D, Foundation Computers, and Computer Animation courses at Tyler, as well as being a CAD/CAM consultant and modeler for several local companies.

His consulting work has him producing jewelry and a line of flatware. Doug has been involved in CAD/CAM since 1995, being introduced to this medium by Stanley Lechtzin at Tyler School of Art. Doug continues to produce a variety of his own original pieces, all of which is made with CAD software. In addition to CAD work, Doug has spent much of his time teaching Metalsmithing and jewelry to a variety of age groups. He has been an instructor of Jewelry, Stonesetting and Lapidary, Metalsmithing, and Blacksmithing.
