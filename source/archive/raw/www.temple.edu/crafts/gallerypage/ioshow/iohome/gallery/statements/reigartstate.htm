April Reigart

Bio:

Born in Pennsylvania in 1975. Grew up around the globe. Earned my BFA in

Metals/Jewelry/CAD-CAM, at Tyler School of Art, in January of 1999. Currently

in my first year of graduate studies at Tyler on a Future Faculty Fellowship.

Statement:

It has been my intention for some years to become a Professor in the field of

the arts. I decided to enroll in Tyler's Graduate Program to pursue this

goal, and to further develop my skills as a craft artist. I chose Tyler

because the program gives me great opportunity to unlock the confines of

creating by using Computer-Aided-Design. I wish to continue to explore the

infinite possibilities offered by CAD; and, I also hope to share these

opportunities, along with the basics of traditional craft, in a classroom.
