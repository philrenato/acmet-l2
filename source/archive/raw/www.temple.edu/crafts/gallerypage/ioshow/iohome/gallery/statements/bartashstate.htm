Adria R. Bartash

 

Adria R. Bartash is a senior at Tyler School of Art majoring in metals,

jewelry, cad/cam. Her pieces integrate playful mechanisms as well as sleek

designs. After graduating this May, she plans to continue on as a designer

using cad/cam as well as bench work.

 

Art has always been my passion. For the past few years I have been privileged

to be able to create three dimensional work in metal. This has taken my art

far beyond what I ever thought possible. Now that I am using the computer to

create my designs, I feel the whole world is at my fingertips.
