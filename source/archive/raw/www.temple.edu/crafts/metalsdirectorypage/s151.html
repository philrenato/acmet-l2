DEGREES

  • Jewelry Technician - a condensed program of 11 classes focused on the essential bench skills needed to enter the field.  This course is preparation for JA Level One: Certified Bench Jeweler Technician.
  • Graduate Jeweler Diploma - a well-rounded program of 22 classes.   The program covers a range of professional skills as a foundation for a career in jewelry.  This course is preparation for JA Level Two: Certified Bench Jeweler.
