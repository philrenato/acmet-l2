# Phil Renato

*formerly Phil Carrizzi*

Phil Renato is a jeweler, metalsmith, and educator based in Grand Rapids, Michigan.
He is a Professor at Kendall College of Art and Design (Ferris State University),
where in 2002 he founded the **Allesee Metals/Jewelry Design** program and served
as its chair. He still teaches there today — though, like the field around it, the
program has changed shape: metals and jewelry is now a concentration inside KCAD's
interdisciplinary **Product Design** BFA rather than a standalone degree.

## Work

Renato's practice moves between studio jewelry, object design, and digital
fabrication. He came up through traditional bench training and then leaned hard
into CAD/CAM — the same lineage the old Tyler "C-C" stood for — using rapid
prototyping and rendering as part of the design language rather than just the
output. His work has appeared in over fifty national and international exhibitions.

## Training

He earned a BFA at **Eastern Michigan University** (1994–1999) studying under Skip
Hunter, and an MFA in Metals at the **University of Washington** (2002) under
**Mary Lee Hu**. He briefly taught at the University of Washington as auxiliary
faculty before taking the position at Kendall.

## The name

Earlier in his career he published and exhibited as **Phil Carrizzi** — the name
under which he appears in the original Academic Metals Directory. He later took the
name **Renato**. The two names are the same person; this is exactly the kind of
quiet change a static directory could never keep up with, and the reason this
project exists.
