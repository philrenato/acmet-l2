# Phil Renato

*formerly Phil Carrizzi*

Phil Renato is a jeweler, metalsmith, and educator based in Grand Rapids,
Michigan. He is a Professor at Kendall College of Art and Design (Ferris State
University), where in 2002 he founded the **Allesee Metals/Jewelry Design**
program and served as its chair. He still teaches there today — though, like the
field around it, the program has changed shape: metals and jewelry is now a
concentration inside KCAD's interdisciplinary **Product Design** BFA rather than
a standalone degree.

## Training

He earned a BFA at **Eastern Michigan University** (1994–1999) studying under Skip
Hunter, and an MFA in Metals at the **University of Washington** (2002) under
**Mary Lee Hu**. He briefly taught at the University of Washington as auxiliary
faculty before taking the position at Kendall.

## The name

The names trace one person: **Rizzi** (1974–2002), then **Carrizzi** (2002–2008)
— the name under which he appears in the original Academic Metals Directory — and
**Renato** since 2008. This is exactly the kind of quiet change a static directory
could never keep up with, and the reason this project exists.
