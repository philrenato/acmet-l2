# Vickie Sedman

Vickie Lee Sedman (b. 1949, Detroit) is a jeweler and silversmith who taught
metals and jewelry at **Tyler School of Art**, Temple University. She is now
Professor Emerita and continues her own studio practice.

## Training

BFA at **Wayne State University** under Philip Fike — the same teacher who trained
Stanley Lechtzin — and MFA at the **University of Wisconsin–Madison** under Arthur
Vierthaler.
