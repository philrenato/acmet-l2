# Skip Hunter

Frederick "Skip" Hunter taught metalsmithing and jewelry at **Eastern Michigan
University**, where he led the metals area for many years before retiring around
2002. He is Professor Emeritus.

## Lineage

He earned his MFA at the **University of Wisconsin–Madison**, studying under the
metalsmith **Fred Fenster** — placing him in one of the central teaching lineages
of American studio metals. At EMU he in turn trained a generation of makers, among
them Phil Renato.
