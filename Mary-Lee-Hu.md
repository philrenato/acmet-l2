# Mary Lee Hu

Mary Lee Hu (b. 1943, Lakewood, Ohio) is one of the defining figures of American
studio jewelry — known for weaving and wrapping fine gold and silver wire with
textile techniques, a language she has worked in since the 1960s.

## Teaching

She taught metals at the **University of Washington** from 1980 until her
retirement in 2006, and is now Professor Emerita. A generation of metalsmiths came
through her classroom — among them Phil Renato, whose MFA she advised.

## Training

BFA at **Cranbrook Academy of Art** under Richard Thomas; further study at the
**Rochester Institute of Technology** under Hans Christensen; MFA at **Southern
Illinois University** under L. Brent Kington.

## Recognition

American Craft Council College of Fellows (1996). In June 2024 she received the
Society of North American Goldsmiths' **Lifetime Achievement Award** in person.
Her work is held in the Smithsonian's Renwick Gallery, the Museum of Arts and
Design, and other major collections.
