What happened
Real Vermont craft school (est. 1948, oldest residential art/craft school in VT, run by Society of Vermont Artists and Craftsmen); now operating as "Create Together at Fletcher Farm" at the same Ludlow, VT address. Currently lists "Jewelry | Metalworking" among studio disciplines with equipped studios; historically offered Introduction to Silversmithing and metal-clay classes. No current named metals/jewelry instructors findable; would not invent. SNAG-listed VT state URL is a directory page that returned 403; own current site is createtogethervt.org.
