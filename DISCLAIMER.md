- Domain-specific outputs (footwear lasts, jewelry geometry, CFD results, literary critiques, etc.) are **exploratory** — do not use them as the basis for manufacturing, legal, medical, financial, or other consequential decisions without independent verification.

**If you use an RDS app, you accept these terms.** If any of the above is a problem for your use case, **do not use the software for that purpose.**
