# Daniella Kerner

Daniella Kerner (b. 1952, Tel Aviv) taught jewelry and metals at **Tyler School of
Art**, Temple University, for some four decades, helping pioneer CAD-CAM jewelry
alongside Stanley Lechtzin. She retired from Tyler in 2018.

## Now

She is **Executive Director of the Long Beach Island Foundation of the Arts and
Sciences** in Loveladies, New Jersey.

## Training

BFA at **Sir George Williams University** (Montreal); MFA at **Tyler School of
Art** under Stanley Lechtzin.
